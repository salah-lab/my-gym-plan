How to use this plan

Train on three nonconsecutive days, such as Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Each exercise displays your prescribed sets and repetitions. The weight and reps fields are your latest log for that exercise, not a per-set history.

*2,200 kcal is a starting estimate, not an exact maintenance number. Check weekly average weight, waist and strength after 3–4 weeks, and adjust if needed. A protein shake is optional if food meets your target. Stop if exercise causes sharp pain.
